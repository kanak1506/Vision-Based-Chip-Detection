## 📁 1. Workspace PDF Papers (Full Text Stored Locally)

These two papers are downloaded and available directly in your project folder:

### 📄 Paper 1: Chip Analysis for Tool Wear Monitoring in Machining: A Deep Learning Approach
* **Local Workspace PDF**: [Open Local PDF File](./Chip_Analysis_for_Tool_Wear_Monitoring_in_Machining_A_Deep_Learning_Approach.pdf)
* **Official Publisher Page**: [IEEE Xplore Article](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10636070)
* **Direct DOI Download Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3443517](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3443517)
* **Authors**: Atiq Ur Rehman, Tahira Salwa Rabbi Nishat, Mobyen Uddin Ahmed, Shahina Begum, Abhishek Ranjan (*SECO Tools AB & Mälardalen University*)
* **Journal**: *IEEE Access*, Vol. 12, 2024
* **Summary**: Uses CNN, AlexNet, ResNet18, MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0, and CoAtNet to classify machining chips into *Accepted*, *Unaccepted*, and *Optimal* tool wear states.

### 📄 Paper 2: Image-based chip detection during turning
* **Local Workspace PDF**: [Open Local PDF File](./Image-based_chip_detection_during_turning.pdf)
* **Official Publisher Page**: [Springer Journal Page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00170-024-14637-x)
* **Direct DOI Download Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-024-14637-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-024-14637-x)
* **Authors**: Tamás Filep, Mátyás Andó, Béla J. Szekeres (*ELTE Eötvös Loránd University*)
* **Journal**: *The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology*, Vol. 135 (2024)
* **Summary**: Camera surveillance setup for turning operations, using Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) tool tracking, edge detection, and background subtraction for live chip segmentation.

* **Summary**: Comprehensive state-of-the-art review on sensor technologies, machine vision, and tool condition monitoring in machining.

### 📄 Paper 4: Study of Using Cutting Chip Color to Tool Wear Prediction
* **Direct Springer Link**: [Read Article on SpringerLink](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00170-020-05354-2)
* **Direct DOI Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-020-05354-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-020-05354-2)
* **Authors**: S. H. Chen, Z. R. Luo
* **Journal**: *The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology*, Vol. 109, pp. 823–839 (2020)

### 📄 Paper 5: Intelligent Tool Wear Monitoring and Multi-Step Prediction Based on Deep Learning
* **Direct ScienceDirect Link**: [Read Article on ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S027861252100238X)
* **Direct DOI Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmsy.2021.12.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmsy.2021.12.012)

* **Journal**: *Journal of Manufacturing Systems*, Vol. 62, pp. 286–300 (2022)

### 📄 Paper 6: Real-Time Industrial Quality Control & Object Detection (YOLO Architectures)
* **Direct ArXiv Abstract Link**: [View on ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09972)

### 📄 Paper 7: Tool Wear Monitoring in Turning Using Image Processing Techniques
* **Direct ScienceDirect Link**: [Read Article on ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S221478532038318X)
* **Direct DOI Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matpr.2020.10.680](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matpr.2020.10.680)
* **Authors**: P. Bagga, M. Makhesana, K. Patel
